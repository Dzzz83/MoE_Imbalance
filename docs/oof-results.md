# OOF Development and Outer Evaluation Results

This document is the concise authoritative record of the completed OOF
development analyses and the single locked outer-fold-0 evaluation. The
protocol and data roles are in [protocol.md](protocol.md); full-data test
results are in [results.md](results.md). The detailed task record and
machine-readable evidence are linked below.

**Conclusion.** Fixed-weight mixtures improved both development metrics over
uniform logits. The locked residual Ridge candidate improved over outer
uniform by point estimate, but its paired intervals include zero and frozen
`fixed_007` and `fixed_010` each exceed it on both BA and Tail. The frozen
expansion gate failed. The result does not establish an adaptive advantage.

## Populations and evidence

The completed collection used seed 78 and outer fold 0. Inner folds 1–3
provided the 6,507-image development population (5,294 Head, 1,030 Medium,
183 Tail). Inner fold 0 contained 2,170 selection rows and was descriptively
inspected in Task 3C before candidate selection. The separate outer
evaluation contained 2,170 rows. Full-data and OOF metrics are not directly
comparable.

The original balanced CIFAR-100 test set was not accessed in the OOF analyses
or locked outer evaluation. Every OOF prediction was generated by an expert
that excluded that image from training. Fit and selection rows are disjoint,
but their experts share training data. This induces dependence between the
fit and selection results.

Detailed task-by-task metrics, diagnostics, provenance, and limitations are in
[the archived Task 3C–3F record](archive/oof-task-history.md). Key immutable
evidence includes the [aligned Task 3C logits](../artifacts/oof/task3c_oof/aligned_oof.npz),
the [Task 3E fixed-weight results](../artifacts/oof/task3e_fixed_feasibility/fixed_weight_results.json),
the [Task 3E soft-feasibility results](../artifacts/oof/task3e_soft_feasibility/soft_oracle_results.json),
the [Task 3F feature comparison](../artifacts/oof/task3f_feature_comparison/diagnostic_results.json),
and the [locked outer results](../artifacts/oof/ridge_sinkhorn_v3/outer_evaluation_v1/results.json).

## Task 3C–3F development findings

### Task 3C: expert complementarity

| Method | BA | Head | Medium | Tail |
|:--|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| CE | 28.5016% | 54.8905% | 23.6979% | 3.3189% |
| LAL | 30.6923% | 49.2259% | 27.4539% | 12.8481% |
| BalancedSoftmax | 31.1445% | 49.5497% | 29.7757% | 11.2688% |
| Mixup | 31.0707% | 61.7710% | 25.8687% | 1.3228% |
| Uniform logits | 35.9328% | 61.9661% | 34.6909% | 7.0094% |
| Uniform probabilities | 35.7778% | 60.9649% | 33.6690% | 8.8532% |
| Hard-selection oracle | 49.5011% | 75.4469% | 48.6691% | 20.2015% |

The hard-selection oracle is label-dependent and not an inference-time
classifier. Among the 4,742 rows with at least one correct expert, exactly one
expert was correct on 974; exclusive correct counts were CE 218, LAL 163,
BalancedSoftmax 154, and Mixup 439. Fourteen Tail classes had no correct
top-1 prediction from any expert in this 183-row population.

### Task 3E: fixed mixtures and soft feasibility

Task 3E-A evaluated the 35 convex logit-weight vectors on a quarter-step
grid, using original logits from the 6,507 development rows. Three improved
both metrics over uniform logits:

| Candidate | Weights (CE, LAL, BalancedSoftmax, Mixup) | BA | Tail |
|:--|:--|--:|--:|
| Uniform logits | (0.25, 0.25, 0.25, 0.25) | 35.9328% | 7.0094% |
| `fixed_006` | (0, 0.25, 0.25, 0.50) | 37.2134% | 9.9115% |
| `fixed_007` | (0, 0.25, 0.50, 0.25) | 36.4403% | 14.7290% |
| `fixed_010` | (0, 0.50, 0.25, 0.25) | 36.4236% | 14.5055% |
| `fixed_011` | (0, 0.50, 0.50, 0) | 35.4372% | 16.7660% |

The observed grid Pareto frontier was `fixed_006`, `fixed_007`, and
`fixed_011`; `fixed_011` trades lower BA for the highest Tail value.
These are development-selected fixed weights, not independently validated
performance.

Task 3E-B solved a separate label-dependent linear program per row to test
whether some convex logit mixture could give the true class a positive margin.
It found 5,063 strictly soft-correctable rows, including 321 (4.9331%) where
every expert's top-1 prediction was wrong, and 1,196 rows misclassified by
uniform logits. Macro feasibility was 55.5569%, with 26.3644% Tail
feasibility; the label-dependent hard-selection oracle had 49.5011% BA and
20.2015% Tail accuracy. Feasibility uses the true label and is not router
accuracy.

### Task 3F: predictable contributions and diagnostics

Task 3F-A evaluated 600 adaptive Ridge configurations and 60 learned-global
controls on the same development population. Sixty adaptive configurations
improved both metrics over uniform logits, but none beat all fixed references
on both BA and Tail. The best adaptive result was 36.5502% BA / 7.3797% Tail;
the best global control was 36.4478% / 6.1297%. The 13-feature model had
lower held-out contribution MSE than confidence-only Ridge (3.9683 versus
4.0126), but this did not yield a fixed-reference advantage.

Task 3F-B found a strong global Mixup preference in the highlighted Ridge:
its Mixup intercept was 1.1009–1.1289, it received the highest weight on
98.38% of Head, 99.51% of Medium, and 99.45% of Tail rows, and its mean
weight was 40.49% on Tail. Retrospectively reducing that weight raised Tail
accuracy at some fixed factors while lowering BA; this did not select a
replacement method.

Task 3F-C and 3F-D found retrospective confidence, disagreement, and
predicted-class-group associations in Tail cases. The combined-signal
analysis tested all 15 predefined masks; smaller subsets sometimes had higher
Tail precision or rebalanced-expert opportunity fractions at lower coverage.
The saved Ridge still gave Mixup the highest weight on 6,415/6,507 rows
(98.59%). No mask or signal became a router feature.

Task 3F-E found that the actual Tail contribution target favored LAL (mean
0.8306) over Mixup (0.6980), but Ridge ranked Mixup above LAL on 102 of the
103 Tail rows where LAL's target was higher. At perturbation `epsilon = 0.5`,
a positive target corrected a wrong uniform prediction in 7.94% of LAL,
8.65% of BalancedSoftmax, and 1.96% of Mixup cases. Positive local log
probability and margin contributions did not reliably change final top-1
classification.

Task 3F-F compared confidence-only with the full 13-feature model across 15
matched settings. Full features reduced pooled contribution MSE in 15/15
settings and Tail MSE in 15/15 (mean full-minus-confidence changes −0.044230
and −0.141768). At the predefined primary setting, confidence-only achieved
36.55% BA / 7.38% Tail, versus 36.10% / 6.55% for full features. Across 300
matched classification comparisons, full features improved BA in 114,
improved Tail in 22, and produced identical top-1 predictions in 60. Better
target prediction did not establish better classification.

## Locked outer-fold-0 evaluation

The selected candidate was confidence-only residual Ridge around
`fixed_007`, with hinge-oracle penalty 1, alpha 0.1, gamma 0, residual scale
1, and no Sinkhorn/OT. Its family and settings were locked before outer
artifacts were loaded. The candidate was refit on all four inner folds before
the single outer evaluation.

| Method | BA | Head | Medium | Tail | Sample accuracy |
|:--|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| Uniform original logits | 42.7006% | 67.3885% | 41.0419% | 15.8333% | 63.9171% |
| Locked residual Ridge, no OT | 44.7035% | 68.4018% | 44.7986% | 16.9444% | 65.1613% |
| `fixed_006` | 44.6348% | 68.1301% | 44.6361% | 17.2222% | 65.1613% |
| `fixed_007` | 44.7248% | 65.3856% | 46.4473% | 18.6111% | 62.7650% |
| `fixed_010` | 44.7298% | 64.1379% | 43.6616% | 23.3333% | 61.8433% |

Relative to uniform, the candidate's BA difference was +2.0028 percentage
points, with paired 95% interval [−0.4956, +4.5771]. Its Tail difference
was +1.1111 points, with interval [−5.0000, +7.7778]. Intervals used 10,000
class/sample hierarchical paired bootstrap replicates with seed 20260924;
both include zero.

Frozen `fixed_007` and `fixed_010` each exceed the candidate on both BA
and Tail. The prespecified new fixed-reference Pareto-point expansion gate
failed, so the full five-fold, three-seed run was not started. Frozen-price
Sinkhorn also failed its development gate and did not advance.

The outer run passed membership, artifact-hash, and metric-reproduction
checks: outer experts trained on 8,677 IDs and predicted the disjoint 2,170
outer IDs, covering all 10,847 canonical IDs together. These integrity checks
do not remove the limits of one seed, one fold, earlier selection-fold
exposure, shared inner-model training dependence, or transfer to full-data
experts.

## Interpretation

The OOF pool has complementary predictions, and fixed composition changes
both BA and Tail. The soft oracle demonstrates feasible headroom, not a way
to find weights at inference time. Across Tasks 3F-A–3F-F, improved target
prediction and retrospective Tail signals did not establish a useful
inference-time contribution predictor. The locked candidate's point estimate
exceeded uniform, but uncertainty includes zero and fixed references
dominated it on both target metrics. No adaptive method passed the frozen
expansion gate.
